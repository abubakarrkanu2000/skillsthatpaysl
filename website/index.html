BEFORE YOU JOIN

A few things
to know.

What does NLe 355 cover?

Your monthly online membership includes all academy courses. Live graphic design classes are a separate NLe 1,450 programme.

When do courses start?

The online academy launches Thursday, 7 January 2027. Live classes also start 7 January 2027; exact teaching days will be announced.

How many places are in the first intake?

The first online intake is limited to 200 learners. Current availability is shown at the top of this page.

Do I need experience?

The introductory courses are designed for beginners. For live graphic design classes, you will need a laptop and access to Photoshop and Illustrator.

Will learning guarantee income?

No. These courses help you build practical foundations. Results depend on your practice, ability, and the opportunities you pursue. Financial markets involve risk.
